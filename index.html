How I think

A good analyst stays for the last mile.

I like the part after the insight: a better experiment, a changed decision, a pipeline that just runs, a teammate who can own it next time.

01 / EVIDENCE

Design for a real comparison.

Controls and baselines turn an encouraging chart into evidence you can make a decision with.

02 / OWNERSHIP

Stay with the full loop.

From metric definition to experiment, forecast, handoff and the next question.

03 / CRAFT

Remove the repetitive work.

Automate carefully, add checks, write the runbook. Let people spend time on judgment.

04 / PRODUCT

Build for the person using it.

Dashboards and tools are products. They need a clear job, a sensible flow and reliable data.

05 / RANGE

Carry ideas between worlds.

Quick commerce, logistics, ride and delivery, and pharma sales teach different ways to find leverage.

06 / PEOPLE

Leave the work stronger.

Mentor, document and make ownership visible. The best system does not depend on one person.
