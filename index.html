What I do differently

  • Prove it with a controlI measure impact against a comparison group, not a before-and-after. At Zepto, retention rose 300 bps and reactivation rose 10% against a control.
  • Own the whole loopI define the metric, design the experiment, forecast the outcome and automate the pipeline. At Swiggy I wrote the Orders per Day and GMV definitions from scratch. At Bolt I run incentive experiments and the quarterly budget.
  • Cut the manual workAt FedEx, four audit pipelines took a two-hour run down to 15 minutes. At ZS, moving Oracle SQL to Alteryx saved 6 days a month.
  • Ship the product, not just the analysisI built Advisoira alone: the site, the ETF, risk and overlap tools, and the data pipelines behind them, in a regulated field.
  • Carry it across marketsQuick commerce, logistics, ride and delivery, and pharma sales across five cities, so a method has to hold up in more than one setting.
  • Make it repeatableI write the SOPs and mentor analysts, and led a 36-person sales team, so the work still runs when I am not in the room.
